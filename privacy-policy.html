Privacy Policy for Daymark

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Overview

Daymark is a fully offline, private photo journal application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information.

Data Collection and Usage

  • No data collection: Daymark does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers.
  • All data stays local: Photos, notes, and journal entries are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to any server.
  • No accounts required: The app does not require registration, login, or any account creation.
  • No third-party services: Daymark does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Permissions

Daymark may request the following permissions solely for core functionality:

  • Camera (via system camera app): To capture photos of the day
  • Photo Library/Gallery: To select photos from your device
  • Notifications: To deliver optional daily reminders
  • Storage access: To save and read photos/notes locally on your device

All permissions are used only locally on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because no data is collected or transmitted.

Data Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or using the app's backup/export features will remove or transfer data as you choose. You have full control over your data.

Children's Privacy

Daymark does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Google Play Store listing.
